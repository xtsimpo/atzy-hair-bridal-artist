Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what happens to the details you send me through the contact form on atzyalafouzou.com. In short: I use them only to reply to you, and I delete them after your wedding day.

Who is responsible for your data

Atzy Alafouzou, bridal hair artist, Greece. You can reach me about anything on this page at:

aggelikialafouzou@gmail.com

What I collect

Only what you type into the contact form:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your wedding date and place, if you add them
  • your message

I don't ask for anything else, and the site doesn't create an account or profile for you.

Why I use it

To reply to your enquiry and, if you book, to plan your trial and your wedding-day styling. The legal basis is that you asked me to get in touch about my services (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps taken at your request before an agreement).

I never use your details for newsletters or marketing, and I never sell or share them with anyone for their own purposes.

How your message reaches me

The form sends your message through Web3Forms, a form delivery service, which forwards it to my email inbox. Web3Forms processes your message only to deliver it. You can read how they handle data in the Web3Forms privacy policy.

Once your message is in my inbox, it is stored in my email account (Gmail) for as long as described below.

How long I keep it

I keep your details until the day of your wedding or event, so I can arrange everything with you. After that day, I delete our correspondence.

Your rights

At any time you can ask me to:

  • tell you what details I hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your details sooner

Just send an email to the address above and I'll take care of it. If you feel your data has not been handled properly, you can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority at dpa.gr.

Cookies and the website itself

This site does not use cookies for analytics or advertising, and it doesn't track you.

Like every website, it loads a few things from other services so it can display properly. When your browser loads them, those services see your IP address:

  • the site is hosted on GitHub Pages
  • the fonts come from Google Fonts
  • the scrolling effects come from cdnjs and unpkg

The site also links to Instagram, Facebook and EasyWedding. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
